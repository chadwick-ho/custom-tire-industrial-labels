Materials

Material and Adhesive Options for Different Surfaces

Material options may include PET, PP, BOPP, vinyl, synthetic paper, coated paper, clear film, white film and other film or paper constructions depending on your application. Adhesive options may include permanent, high tack, removable and application-specific adhesive constructions. Available constructions should be confirmed for each project.

Material and adhesive recommendations depend on application surface and environmental requirements.

Variable Data

Barcode, QR Code and Variable Data Label Printing

Many tire and industrial label projects require unique information on each label. We can discuss labels with barcodes, QR codes, serial numbers, batch numbers, product codes, model numbers and variable text. Please send your data file, numbering rule or barcode requirement for review.
