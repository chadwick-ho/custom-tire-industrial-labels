Durable Label Applications

Special Environment Labels

Special environment labels are custom labels for demanding surfaces and use conditions where moisture, abrasion, outdoor exposure, temperature, cleaning, handling or chemical contact may need review. The suitable construction depends on the actual application, not a generic product claim.

Quote Checklist

  • Label type and application
  • Surface and environment
  • Size, shape and quantity
  • Material and adhesive preference
  • Barcode, QR code or variable data
  • Artwork or existing label reference

Demanding Use Conditions

Use this category when the label must be discussed around exposure and handling requirements.

  • Moisture exposure
  • Outdoor use
  • Abrasion or frequent handling
  • Temperature variation
  • Cleaning or wiping
  • Textured or curved surfaces
  • Chemical contact review

What to Send

Send photos of the application surface, expected environment, label size, quantity, desired material, adhesive requirement, artwork and any required performance specification.

Proof and Sample Discussion

A digital proof confirms the visual layout. Physical performance should be evaluated with the selected construction and actual application conditions when required.
