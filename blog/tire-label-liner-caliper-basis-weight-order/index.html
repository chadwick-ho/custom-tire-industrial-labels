Tire Label Liner Specifications: Record Thickness Separately From Basis Weight

Published November 15, 2026

A converter quotes an alternative backing for a tire label roll. The current machine brief states a thickness limit, while the offer names a paper weight in grams per square metre. Purchasing needs both parties to identify the property they are comparing before accepting the substitution.

Keep the units and the layer name visible

Ask for the liner’s construction reference, material type, basis weight and caliper where available. Name the layer each figure describes. A backing-only thickness differs from the full face, adhesive and liner stack passing through a machine. The supplier should identify typical values and tolerances separately from a guaranteed order limit.

Do not convert a mass-per-area figure to thickness without an appropriate material basis. Even when two papers have similar weight, their density and construction may differ. Have the equipment owner compare the actual complete stock with its media-path requirement. Then witness feed, detection and release on a representative converted roll, because thickness alone does not establish those behaviors.

Avery Dennison’s Rapid Roll product guide defines basis weight as mass per area and caliper as thickness. It also identifies its listed data as typical values.

Review pointEvidence to keep
PropertyBasis weight, caliper and their explicit units.
ScopeLiner alone or the full construction through the machine.
SupplyMaterial reference, document revision and tolerance or limit status.
AcceptanceEquipment requirement and a representative converted-roll witness.

Before approving the order

Hold if an offer only supplies a weight where the machine requires a thickness. Request the missing property and avoid treating an unknown value as zero. For installed clearance on the tire, use the label construction after the backing is removed.

Related reading includes the roll dimension guide, the installed label thickness guide, the liner-release guide.

For tire tread labels, send the machine’s stated media limits, current and proposed liner references and the required roll drawing.
