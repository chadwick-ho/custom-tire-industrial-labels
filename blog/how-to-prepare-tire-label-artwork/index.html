How to Prepare Tire Label Artwork

Clear artwork and data files help speed up digital proofing for custom tire labels. Finished artwork is helpful, but a reference label or photo can also start the discussion.

Send Your Label RequirementsDiscuss Your ApplicationChat with a Label Specialist

Digital proof before production. Material and adhesive options are discussed according to your application requirements.

Quote Checklist

  • Label type and application
  • Surface and environment
  • Size, shape and quantity
  • Material and adhesive preference
  • Barcode, QR code or variable data
  • Artwork or existing label reference
Send Requirements

What to Include

Include label size, product text, tire specification, brand information, barcode or QR code requirements and any color or finish preferences.

Variable Data

If each label needs unique data, send an Excel or CSV file with product codes, serial numbers, batch numbers or barcode content.

Digital Proof

A digital proof confirms layout, text, size and barcode placement before production. It does not confirm adhesion or durability.

FAQ

No. Actual material performance should be confirmed based on the selected construction and application requirements.
