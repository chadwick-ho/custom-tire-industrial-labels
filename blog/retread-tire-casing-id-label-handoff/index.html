Retread Casing Labels: Keep the Casing ID Through the Handoff

Published November 11, 2026

A fleet sends casings to a retread partner. The partner assigns a work-order reference, and later the same casing may return for another service job. If the latest job number replaces the casing ID, staff can lose the relationship between the physical item and earlier records.

Identify the casing and the service event separately

Michelin's commercial retread program information describes tracking casing location and status. It illustrates the business purpose of keeping casing records visible. It does not prescribe a universal label, a traceability schema or a construction that survives a retread process.

Ask the fleet which record identifies the casing and ask the service partner what its job number identifies. A job may cover more than one casing; a casing may appear in more than one job. If a job line distinguishes individual items, record it with the job. Keep any original manufacturer markings in their own controlled fields.

Agree the crosswalk at each physical handoff

HandoffDecision to retain
Fleet dispatchApproved casing reference, dispatch record and authorized capture method.
Partner intakeLink from casing reference to service job and item line where used.
Process boundaryWho preserves identity if a temporary label must be removed or replaced.
ReturnResponsible owner confirms the returned casing and the related service record.

In a fictional example, casing CASE-A is first linked to job J-31, then later to J-82. Both entries can remain valid for their own service events. The examples are invented references, not identifiers or records from a customer.

Define where the physical label is used

Confirm which stages allow the proposed label and which require a different identification method. A sticker suitable for a receiving bench may be unsuitable for cleaning, buffing or other processing. Do not carry an ordinary logistics sticker into a production step unless the process owner has approved that specific use. This guide offers no process-survival claim.

If identification becomes uncertain, hold the affected item for the fleet or service owner. Do not reconstruct identity from tire size alone. Conversely, if an existing approved mark and record already support reliable handoff, a duplicate physical label may be unnecessary.

For supplemental tire tracking labels, send the permitted application stages, casing surface, approved example data, reader details and quantity. The used-tire guide covers the separate distinction between identifying an item and judging its condition.
