Quick Answer for Electrical Label Buyers

For electrical rating labels, do not begin with the label material alone. First confirm the required rating information, symbols, certification marks, model number, serial data, barcode or QR code and available label area. Then discuss material, adhesive, finish and printing format.

  • CONTENT: voltage, current, model, warning text, symbols and brand information
  • DATA: serial number, barcode, QR code, batch or date code
  • READABILITY: small text size, contrast, spacing and proof review
  • SURFACE: plastic housing, metal panel, appliance body or control component

Small Text Is the Main Design Constraint

Electrical labels often carry a lot of information in a small area. If text is too small or crowded, the label may look cheap and become hard to inspect. A good proof should show text hierarchy, line spacing, barcode area and whether the most important rating data can be read at the intended size.

Certification Marks and Responsibility

Some rating labels include marks, symbols or approval references requested by the buyer. The label supplier can print provided artwork, but the buyer should confirm whether those marks are allowed for the product and market. Do not treat a printed mark as proof of certification unless the buyer has the supporting approval.

Serial Number and Variable Data

Electrical rating labels often need changing serial numbers, batch codes, model numbers or barcode data. For variable data printing, send an Excel or CSV file with exact fields, prefixes, leading zeros and sample rows. The proof should include sample data placement before bulk production.

Material, Finish and Adhesive

Rating labels may be applied to plastic housings, metal panels, appliances or control boxes. PET, PP, BOPP, synthetic paper or other constructions may be discussed according to surface, exposure and readability requirements. Matte or gloss finish should be considered alongside barcode scanning and small text clarity.

FAQ

Questions Buyers Ask

Can electrical rating labels include unique serial numbers?

Yes. Variable serial numbers, model data, barcodes and QR codes can be discussed when you provide a clear data file.

Can you print UL, CE or other symbols on rating labels?

We can print buyer-supplied artwork, but the buyer should confirm the right to use any certification or compliance mark for the product and target market.

What affects small text readability on rating labels?

Label size, font size, contrast, material finish, print method, artwork quality and available space all affect readability.
