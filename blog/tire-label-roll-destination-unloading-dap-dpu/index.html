Receiving Pallets of Tire Label Rolls: Compare DAP and DPU at the Same Dock

Published November 17, 2026

A tire label buyer sends one delivery address to two suppliers. The cartons will be palletized, and the receiving site has no staffed loading bay every day. Confirm the exact place and the unloading arrangement before comparing delivered prices or booking a dock slot.

Price the equipment and the handoff at the same point

Under Incoterms 2020 DAP, the delivery condition at the named point is goods at the buyer’s disposal on the arriving transport, ready to unload. Under DPU, the seller delivers after unloading at the stated point. Ask the parties to confirm the exact direction, site and point in the sales agreement. At one address, a gate, loading bay and warehouse door can represent very different practical arrangements.

Give both suppliers the same packed quantity and pallet dimensions. Ask who schedules the dock, provides an authorized unloading method, and records handover. Under DAP, verify how any unloading charge under the carriage agreement is handled separately from the delivery condition. Confirm import responsibilities with your trade team and record them outside the physical dock checklist. A courier carton shipment can have different handling needs from a pallet arriving on a vehicle.

ICC’s comparison of DAP and DPU explains the different delivery and risk points before and after unloading. Confirm the written contract and the practical site arrangement for the order.

What to include in the approval record

Before approving the order

Hold a price comparison if the actual receiving method is unknown or one supplier priced a different point. The receiving team should agree that the promised handoff is possible at the site before purchasing releases the order.
