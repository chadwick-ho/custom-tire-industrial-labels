A Strong Label Film Can Still Need a Separate Tear-Resistance Review

Published November 20, 2026

A narrow industrial label splits while an operator peels or handles it. The substitute film has a higher tensile-strength number, so it appears to be an obvious upgrade. First identify whether the observed failure is an intact strip stretching apart or an existing edge defect growing through the label. Those observations call for different evidence.

Keep the failure mode and film direction on the same record

Retain a questioned specimen and record where the split begins, the handling step, supplied construction and orientation on the original web. Photograph any cut, nick or sharp internal feature without creating a new defect to explain the old one. If the liner or adhesive is failing instead of the face, preserve that distinction before comparing face-film figures.

Tensile strength describes a breaking stress under a stated tensile test; elongation describes extension before break under its method. A tear result describes resistance to a defined tearing action. Do not rank them together merely because all appear under mechanical properties. Ask for the actual method, specimen thickness, conditioning, direction, units and status of the reported value.

Keep machine direction and transverse direction identifiable after slitting and cutting. They are material directions, not automatically the width and height of the installed artwork. A revised unwind or rotated layout can change which material direction lies across a narrow section. Have the converter map the proposed supply to the drawing and let the technical reviewer select comparable evidence.

Avery Dennison’s Rapid-Roll high-strength applications guide distinguishes tensile strength, elongation and tear properties of unsupported materials. Those screening properties do not qualify a printed adhesive construction or establish that CYJ stocks a named film.

What to include in the approval record

Review pointEvidence to keep
FailureSpecimen, start location and actual handling action.
Material directionMD/TD mapped from supplied web to finished label.
Comparable evidenceSame property, method, thickness basis, units and conditions.
Finished trialRepresentative cut/print construction through the intended handling task.

Before approving the order

Hold a substitution based solely on a larger tensile number. Review the cutting and handling evidence as well as the material, then trial the proposed finished label. A deliberately destructible security label has a different required opening behavior; greater tear resistance is not automatically an improvement for that job.
