Separate the label dimensions from the supplied web

A label's outside width and height do not fully describe the material entering a printer or dispenser. Record the finished label separately from the wider carrier web and from the complete construction passing through the approved media path.

ItemObtain from the actual project
Finished labelWidth, length in feed direction, shape and approved tolerances
Carrier webTotal width, edge margins and registration features
RepeatPitch and gap in the stated feed direction
ConstructionRelevant layer identities and complete caliper where required
EquipmentExact installed model and applicable media limits

Zebra's GX-series specifications provide a model-specific example of separate media dimensions and thickness limits. Do not apply that model's numerical limits to another device. The equipment owner must confirm the offered web and finished construction against the actual installed configuration.

A thickness stated for face film alone is not automatically the complete label-and-liner caliper. Identify the basis rather than adding nominal figures and calling the result a measured specification. Use a supplier-confirmed construction record and any agreed actual measurement where the device fit depends on it.

Send the controlled web drawing, construction reference, equipment requirements and quantity with the enquiry. Resolve missing dimensions or an unsupported substitution before bulk approval. The automatic-roll guide adds the separate sensing and application checks. Source checked November 7, 2026.
