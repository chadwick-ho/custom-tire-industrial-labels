Short Industrial Labels: Check Minimum Length in the Intended Print Mode

Published November 11, 2026

A small component label fits the part and has a readable proof. It prints during a test with the media collected on a roll, but production needs the machine to present labels in a different output mode. Check the minimum length for that mode before approving the finished die size.

Put the feed-direction length on the drawing

Mark the leading and trailing edges, the direction of feed, the gap and the repeat pitch. Do not rely on width by height shorthand when the physical orientation has not been agreed. Ask the printer owner which dimension the applicable manual measures for its limit.

Zebra's ZT510 technical specifications list different minimum label lengths by mode. For example, they list 6 mm for rewind and 13 mm for peel. On that published comparison, a 10 mm example exceeds the stated rewind minimum but falls below the peel minimum. It does not prove that the example meets other requirements or that these limits apply to any other printer.

Test the job that will run in production

A label that prints in one mode does not automatically qualify in another. Have the equipment owner review the same representative media and released job in the required mode. Record the specimen and output behavior. The geometry check should be followed by the relevant read or apply check.

For a cutter job, minimum cut interval and permitted layers may impose separate limits. For a rotated label, changing the feed dimension may also change the usable print area, scan orientation or placement. Do not use rotation as a workaround before the related owners approve those effects.

Hold the die size when the output mode is unknown

If the required mode or hardware is unresolved, avoid committing to the final roll. A permitted alternative orientation, another construction or an approved different handoff may solve the issue. Compare those options without assuming the device can be adjusted outside its requirements.

For small industrial barcode labels, send the printer and installed option, required output mode, marked web drawing, usable part area and quantities. Use the cutter guide for the separate check of what material the blade would cross.
