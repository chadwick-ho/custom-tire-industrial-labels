Equipment Shipped in Separate Pieces: Match Each Piece Label to the Trade Item

Published November 17, 2026

A supplier sells one configured piece of equipment. The machine body and a separately packed enclosure travel as two units. Receiving should be able to tell which piece is on each unit and whether the equipment set is complete. Clarify whether the trading partners use GS1 identification and how they represent the relationship in their receiving systems before printing.

Agree on both the piece and the logistic unit

Ask the product owner to confirm the trade item identity and the number of pieces in the approved configuration. The GS1 Logistic Label Guideline describes AI (8006) for an individual trade item piece. Its field combines the trade item GTIN, a two-digit piece number and a two-digit total. The corresponding logistic label example also identifies the physical logistic unit with an SSCC.

Give receiving samples in both expected arrival orders. Check how it records the piece and the physical unit and what status it gives an incomplete set. A readable piece description helps staff reconcile a unit with an exception record. If several pieces are packed together or pieces of different trade items share a unit, review the applicable scenario before reusing the individual-piece artwork. If a component is an independent trade item, have the owner review the right identifier for that component.

The GS1 Logistic Label Guideline, sections 4.3 and 10.14 covers the separately shipped piece scenario and illustrates the use of both ITIP and SSCC. Confirm which fields and carriers the trading partner accepts.

What to include in the approval record

Review pointEvidence to keep
Parent recordOwner-approved trade item and its configuration reference.
Packing mapPiece positions and totals by configuration, with a physical unit assignment.
Label dataApproved piece field, unit identifier and readable description for each print.
Receiving proofEach unit’s stored link to the correct piece and the outcome when a piece is missing.

Before approving the order

Hold if a piece label has the right item but the wrong ordinal, or receiving cannot associate a piece with what remains to be received. A closed workflow using its own packing references can check the same relationship without adopting another numbering system.

Related reading includes the separately identified component comparison, the service kit guide, the logistic unit and transport-reference guide.

For industrial barcode qr code labels, send the packing map, approved identifier fields, representative unit records and label dimensions.
