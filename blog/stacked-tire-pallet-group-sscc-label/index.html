Stacked Tire Pallets: Define the Group Label Before Wrapping

Published November 12, 2026

A distributor prepares two identified tire pallets. The logistics team later joins them for shipment and asks for an outer barcode. First decide whether the two pallets still ship and scan separately or whether the receiver will treat the joined load as an additional logistics unit.

Release the group and the child records together

For a GS1 workflow, ask the responsible data owner to confirm the approved identifiers and the relationship between the units. Identify the child pallets before they are joined. If the trading process uses a group SSCC, match it to the exact child list in the dispatch or receiving data. Keep the group and individual tire SKU records distinct.

At dispatch, verify the identifier the receiver will see in the group state. At deconsolidation, verify that each child pallet remains identifiable. Preserve the child label’s function for the intended stage and ask the logistics owner to define visibility on the actual wrapped load. If the group is broken or rebuilt before shipment, have that owner review the revised record before labels are reused.

Section 8.3 of the GS1 Logistic Label Guideline distinguishes stacked independent pallets from pallets physically joined for shipment as a unit. It describes an additional group SSCC and an outer master label in the joined case.

Review pointEvidence to record before ordering
Dispatch stateWhether pallets are independent or physically joined for receipt as one unit.
Approved mappingGroup SSCC where applicable, exact child pallet list and corresponding dispatch data.
WitnessVisible outer label on the finished group and readable child labels after the approved unwrapping step.

When to release the order

Hold the master artwork if group membership or the expected receiving scan is unresolved. The logistics and data owners release the mapping. For pallets shipped as independent units, retain their separate identifiers and follow the partner’s handling instructions.
