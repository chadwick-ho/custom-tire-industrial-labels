Tire Label Material Considerations

Tire label material selection should begin with the actual tire surface, application method, printed information and handling requirements. Different tire projects may require different label constructions.

Send Your Label RequirementsDiscuss Your ApplicationChat with a Label Specialist

Digital proof before production. Material and adhesive options are discussed according to your application requirements.

Quote Checklist

  • Label type and application
  • Surface and environment
  • Size, shape and quantity
  • Material and adhesive preference
  • Barcode, QR code or variable data
  • Artwork or existing label reference
Send Requirements

Start With the Surface

Tell your supplier whether the label will be applied to tread, sidewall, packaging or warehouse handling materials. Surface texture and application process can affect material and adhesive discussion.

Send the Use Conditions

If the label may face moisture, oil, abrasion, transport handling or outdoor exposure, include those details in the inquiry. Actual material performance should be confirmed based on the selected construction and application requirements.

Prepare Print Information

Provide product text, barcode data, QR code data, tire size, product code, batch information and artwork if available.
