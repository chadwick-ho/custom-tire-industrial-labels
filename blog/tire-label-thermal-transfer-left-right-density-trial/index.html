Thermal Transfer Tire Label Trials: Compare Print Density Across the New Roll Width

Published November 17, 2026

A plant changes from a proven narrow tire label roll to a wider one. The code or text on one side looks consistently lighter, even though its printed position is correct. Purchasing needs to know whether the proposed width can pass on the installed equipment before changing the label stock or committing to a larger order.

Compare equivalent print features across the web

Use a representative job plus a witness carrying the same test feature on both sides of the web. Its purpose is to separate a side-to-side difference from an artwork change. Mark the inboard and outboard positions and retain successive labels after the machine owner decides the job is steady. Keep the effective speed, darkness, stock, ribbon and approved print configuration on the sample record.

Check the actual device manual before treating a recurring light side as a material defect. Zebra identifies uneven pressure as one possibility on certain models. Have the equipment owner evaluate the pattern and compare any approved setting or hardware change with the same trial job. If both sides improve or the pattern changes, retain the original and revised witness. Evaluate the areas that carry required text or barcodes when deciding whether the finished labels are usable.

Zebra’s printhead pressure guidance for specified ZT models lists one-sided light print as a reason to review the setup. The buyer’s equipment owner should use the correct procedure for the actual machine.

What to include in the approval record

Review pointEvidence to keep
WebApproved face and liner selection, finished and web widths, and ribbon reference.
OrientationIdentify the same inside and outside sides on each comparison sample.
WitnessEquivalent cross-web print features, repeated through the approved workflow.
ResultMachine owner disposition, affected label fields and evidence of finished-label quality.

Before approving the order

Hold a material substitution if the available evidence comes from one isolated sample or from different artwork at each side. A placement error with otherwise even print belongs with the cross-web registration check.

Related reading includes the intermittent void evidence guide, the print settings approval guide, the print placement guide.

For tire barcode labels, send the finished widths, printer model, ribbon and media references, and repeated photos marked on both sides.
