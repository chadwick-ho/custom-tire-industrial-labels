Parcel Quotes for Tire Label Rolls: Measure the Packed Cartons

Published November 8, 2026

A purchasing team compares parcel quotes for the same tire-label roll order. One estimate uses the roll weights from production. The other uses carton dimensions and packed weights. Before comparing the charges, give both parcel services the same measured packing information.

FedEx's dimensional-weight explanation describes comparing a package's size-based weight with its actual weight. UPS also describes its billable weight process and points to service-specific rules. Obtain the applicable measurement, divisor, rounding and pricing terms from the actual carrier or logistics provider.

Measure each carton as dispatched

Record the outside length, width and height for each packed carton. Weigh the carton with roll cores, any support and all protective materials included. If only a packing estimate is available when quotes are requested, mark the figures estimated and recheck the actual packed cartons before release. Keep the number of rolls and SKU allocation with each carton reference.

Compare the inputs before comparing the amounts

Review itemWhat to provide
Carton dataOutside dimensions and gross weight for each package, with units.
ServiceExact origin, destination, service and the carrier's current calculation basis.
ProtectionAgreed roll support, label and carton identity, and any required handling method.
QuoteValid dates, included charges and how multiple parcels are rated.

In a hypothetical example, assume a divisor of 5,000 cm³/kg and ignore rounding to show the calculation. An outer carton of 50 × 40 × 30 cm gives 12 kg of dimensional weight. With an actual packed mass of 9 kg, the example uses 12 kg if the rule selects the greater value. Another carton measuring 45 × 40 × 25 cm gives 9 kg of dimensional weight and still has 9 kg of actual mass. These are fictional inputs and calculation assumptions. Obtain the confirmed package and pricing rules for the route.

A lower calculated weight is useful only when the alternative protects the rolls and fits the receiving plan. Check whether a proposed carton split changes package count, roll support, receiving labels or service charges. For cost boundaries beyond the parcel calculation, use the tire label delivery scope guide.

For tire label roll supply, send the quantities and roll dimensions, destination and required packing conditions. Ask for the proposed packed carton data and have the relevant carrier or logistics owner confirm the quoted basis.
