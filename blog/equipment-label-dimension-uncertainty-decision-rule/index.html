Equipment Label Dimensions: Agree the Decision Rule for Results Near a Limit

Published November 24, 2026

The equipment drawing sets a maximum finished-label width. Receiving measures a sample close to that limit, while the report includes measurement uncertainty. Before one team accepts the roll and another rejects it, agree the decision rule and the measurement basis with the responsible quality owner.

Keep the specification, result and decision separate

The tolerance describes allowable dimensions. Measurement uncertainty characterizes uncertainty associated with the reported measurement; it is not extra manufacturing allowance. Do not add the reported uncertainty to the drawing limit and call the product acceptable, or subtract it automatically and reject the order without the required decision basis.

Consider fictional values only: an upper specification limit of 50.00 mm, a measured value of 49.96 mm and a stated expanded uncertainty of 0.08 mm. An illustrated symmetric interval would span 49.88 to 50.04 mm. The central value is below the upper limit while that interval crosses it. Those observations do not choose the contractual acceptance rule.

Have the quality owner identify the applicable decision rule, any prescribed standard and the required measurement capability. Ask the report issuer what its uncertainty statement covers, including the coverage factor or coverage information as applicable. A drawing tolerance and a calibration certificate alone may not define uncertainty for measuring this flexible finished label in this state.

Agree the measured feature and state before collecting more readings: width between which edges, on or off the liner, after which conditioning, and with what method. Repeated measurements on differently defined features cannot resolve a disagreement about the acceptance rule. Preserve the original results and refer a disputed disposition to the named owner.

JCGM 106:2012, published by BIPM addresses measurement uncertainty in conformity assessment and the role of decision rules. This guide does not prescribe a universal guard band or replace the measurement procedure required for an order.

What to include in the approval record

Review pointEvidence to keep
SpecificationDrawing revision, measured feature and allowable limits.
MeasurementSpecimen state, method, result, units and sample identity.
UncertaintyIssuer’s stated uncertainty and its coverage information, relevant to this measurement.
DispositionAgreed decision rule, responsible quality owner and recorded decision.

Before approving the order

Hold the disputed release when the decision rule or measurement definition is missing. Resolve those inputs before demanding a tighter cutting tolerance or using a different number of decimal places. A preliminary physical fit trial answers a different question from a documented dimensional conformity decision.

Related reading includes the limiting-size fit check, the specimen-state guide.

For equipment machinery labels, send the controlled drawing, receiving requirement, specimen state and available measurement report with the proposed label construction and quantities.
