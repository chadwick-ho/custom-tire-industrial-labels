Equipment Heat Exposure: Specify the Indicator and the Durable ID Separately

Published November 13, 2026

A buyer requests a heat-resistant equipment label and expects it to show whether a handling limit was exceeded. That request combines a durable identification job and an exposure evidence job. Define which result the operator needs from each part of the marking.

Choose the record each label must support

Specify which ID or instruction must remain legible through the expected exposure. Ask the equipment owner to define the actual surface, location and service conditions. If evidence of a threshold is needed, ask the indicator supplier which exposure their specific product can show, what it requires for placement and when it must be inspected or replaced. A temperature at the label position need not represent a different part of the assembly.

Plan how the operator will record the observation against the correct equipment ID. Where a time history or traceable process record is required, involve the quality owner in selecting the proper measurement system. A visible state change alone cannot establish an entire time-temperature profile.

SpotSee’s irreversible indicator overview describes products that retain a visible change at a defined threshold. Select and validate the indicator with its supplier for the actual process.

Review pointEvidence to keep
Durable IDApproved data, location, substrate, expected exposure and legibility check.
WitnessSupplier reference, approved threshold and placement, inspection and replacement process.
RecordNamed equipment ID, time of observation, observed state and authorized disposition.

Before approving the order

Hold if the permanent label and the witness are specified as a single unsupported claim. For identification only, qualify the durable label against its service conditions. If a witness is also required, obtain it through the approved process and keep its evidence linked to the item.

For equipment machinery labels, send the equipment surface photo, heat exposure, required permanent data, intended placement and quantities.
