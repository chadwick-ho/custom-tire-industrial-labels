Old SSCC Label Stock Needs an Allocation Check Before the Next Tire Pallet

Published November 21, 2026

A warehouse finds a pack of preprinted SSCC labels beside the tire-pallet station. Some may be reserved and never issued; others may be extra copies from an earlier shipment. Their physical condition does not establish their allocation status. Check the exact references before using the pack on new pallets.

Compare each printed reference with the authoritative ledger

Ask the identifier owner to distinguish never-issued reservations, spare copies still tied to an identified unit, and numbers previously assigned to another unit. Record the exact source range and its issuer. An old print date, an unopened bag or a valid check digit cannot prove that a number is available for a new assignment.

The current GS1 General Specifications state that an SSCC remains with its logistics unit for that unit's life and must not be reallocated within one year of shipment from the assignor to a trading partner; applicable sector or regulatory requirements can extend that period. A longer applicable restriction still needs to be checked. Elapsed time alone does not establish that retained systems and partners will handle another assignment as intended.

GS1's October 2026 change notification 26-163 adds encouragement to use available SSCCs before reuse, to extend unambiguous traceability. The change directory lists it for version 27.0. Treat the revision and the currently applicable requirements explicitly, without describing the update as a universal new lifetime ban. Have the authorized owner approve the actual release policy and the specific numbers, then reconcile new physical units with their dispatch records.

GS1’s SSCC reallocation change notification shows the additional non-reuse guidance alongside the existing period. Use the applicable General Specifications and receiving requirements to establish the actual allocation decision.

What to include in the approval record

Review pointEvidence to keep
Stock identityExact printed numbers, source pack and issuer.
Ledger stateNever issued, reserved, issued to a known unit or previously used.
Applicable policyAllocation authority, prior shipment evidence and relevant restrictions.
ReleaseAuthorized references, physical new units and matching dispatch records.

Before approving the order

Hold anonymous or unreconciled stock. A verified spare for the same intact unit may be a replacement copy; it is not a new-unit allocation. Where unused authorized numbers are available, the owner can consider them before a reuse review. Keep rejected stock identifiable under the site's disposition process.

Related reading includes the intact-unit replacement guide, the general reserved-range guide, the rebuilt-unit release guide.

For tire barcode labels, send a redacted stock-range record, allocation states, intended unit count, receiver requirements and physical label format.
