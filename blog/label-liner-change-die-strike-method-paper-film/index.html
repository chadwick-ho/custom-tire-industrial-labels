Changing a Label Liner Requires a Die-Strike Review for the New Construction

Published November 18, 2026

A converter proposes a new release liner for an established tire label. The face shape and tooling reference stay the same. Before ordering converted rolls, ask how the supplier will confirm the cut is suitable for the new complete construction and the intended dispensing process.

Specify the evidence before the conversion trial

Identify the face, adhesive, liner and intended cut format on one construction drawing. A cut that releases the label face still needs to leave the supporting web suitable for its job. Ask the converter to identify an applicable die-strike method and the specimen locations and acceptance criteria it proposes. Keep the method’s revision and limitations with the trial record.

FINAT lists separate die-strike methods for paper liners and clear film liners. A paper-liner observation should therefore retain its scope when a supplier proposes clear film. Connect the report to representative converted samples from the trial and to the equipment owner’s approved dispensing witness. Retain any unexplained cut pattern or liner weakness as an exception. A clean face outline alone cannot release the supporting web for automatic use.

FINAT’s technical handbook overview lists FTM 23a for paper liners and FTM 23b for clear filmic liners. The overview identifies the methods and does not supply a universal acceptance threshold.

What to include in the approval record

Review pointEvidence to keep
ConstructionReleased face, adhesive, liner and cut-format references.
MethodApplicable die-strike method, revision and agreed acceptance responsibility.
SpecimensPositions sampled from the conversion trial and retained pieces.
Use witnessDispensing evidence on the intended line and approval for the changed construction.

Before approving the order

Hold a liner substitution when the existing report covers a different construction or the cut evidence is unresolved. When the unchanged construction and process remain within the existing approved scope, retain that scope with the reorder.

For tire tread labels, send the old and proposed liner references, die-cut drawing, converted samples and intended dispensing equipment.
