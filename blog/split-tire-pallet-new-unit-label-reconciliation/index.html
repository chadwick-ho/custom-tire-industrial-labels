Splitting a Tire Pallet Requires a New-Unit Label Plan

Published November 21, 2026

A distributor receives a tire pallet and rebuilds its contents into two outbound logistics units. Printing the original pallet's label twice would give two new units the same identity. Before ordering or printing the outbound labels, have logistics release the new units and preserve their relationship to the received load.

Reconcile the physical split before releasing label data

Record the original unit reference and counted contents before breakdown. Name the new physical units, their destinations and the actual quantities transferred into each. Keep any remaining or held tires visible in the reconciliation instead of forcing the two outbound totals to match the original count. The unit-building owner should define which identities the agreed logistics scheme requires.

For GS1 workflows, have the authorized identifier owner allocate the new-unit SSCCs and retain their relationship to the original unit. Do not derive a new SSCC by appending a letter to the old one or treat a valid check digit as allocation authority. Each final label must correspond to its own released unit and contents record. A readable internal reference can support the handling record but should not be presented as an SSCC unless it actually meets that scheme.

Compare the final physical units, printed and decoded IDs and receiving data together before dispatch. Ask the record owner to update the applicable dispatch advice or other partner record through the agreed process. Keep superseded labels from routing a new unit under the original identity. When one unit needs labels on two faces, those copies represent that one unit, not two separately assigned units.

GS1 Portugal’s logistics-label guide, section 3.2 distinguishes an intact logistics unit from a broken and reconstituted unit, with new identifiers and links between them. This national guide illustrates the identity decision; confirm the current applicable partner requirements.

What to include in the approval record

Review pointEvidence to keep
Original unitReceived identity and counted SKU/quantity record.
Rebuilt unitsActual unit boundaries, destinations and allocated quantities.
Identity releaseAuthorized new IDs and retained original-to-new relationships.
Dispatch witnessPhysical units, exact decoded IDs and partner records reconciled.

Before approving the order

Hold outbound label release if quantities do not reconcile or two new units share the old unit's code. An intact pallet with only a carrier change follows a different replacement decision. For a private internal unit-ID scheme, use its owner's allocation rules and the receiver's approved mapping.

Related reading includes the mixed-load contents guide, the joined-pallet group guide, the intact-unit carrier change guide.

For tire barcode labels, send a redacted original-to-new unit list, SKU counts, approved identifier fields, receiving requirements and physical label dimensions.
