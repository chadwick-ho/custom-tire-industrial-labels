Printed Equipment Labels Before Lamination: Confirm the Process Release

Published November 15, 2026

A new equipment label proof has the correct artwork and colour. The converter proposes adding the protective layer immediately to meet dispatch timing. Ask how the actual print system establishes readiness for that next step, especially when the ink, film or coating has changed.

Match the evidence to the actual ink process

Request an identifiable ink and film combination and the supplier’s approved process instructions. For solvent-printed media, ask what drying or solvent-release condition the provider requires before covering the print. For a UV-cured system, ask how the converter confirms the cure condition and adhesion under its actual production setup. A familiar elapsed time or a surface that feels dry cannot resolve every print process.

Keep the print and finishing references linked to the sample. Ask which process changes trigger another check, and preserve the evidence supporting the next-step release. Witness the complete finished construction against the buyer’s actual readability and handling criteria. The converter should control production settings and testing under the appropriate ink and film instructions.

ORAFOL’s solvent-printed graphics guidance discusses drying before lamination for its stated materials and process. Its conditions should be assessed against the actual quoted media.

Nazdar’s 4000 UV screen ink data sheet explains the need to establish cure under actual production conditions and check the cooled print. Its guidance applies to that ink system.

Review pointEvidence to keep
ConstructionInk, face material and proposed protective layer references.
ProcessRelevant supplier instructions and actual production route.
Release evidenceAppropriate drying or cure and adhesion evidence before finishing.
Final witnessFinished sample, readable fields, handling criteria and approval owner.

Before approving the order

Hold the finishing release when the supplier cannot identify the applicable process evidence. A dye, solvent, water-based or UV workflow should be evaluated under its own instructions. Allow the confirmed production requirements to inform the delivery plan.

Related reading includes the finish selection guide, the colour-ink durability guide, the curl observation guide.

For equipment machinery labels, send the approved artwork, finish request, actual handling conditions and the converter’s proposed print process.
