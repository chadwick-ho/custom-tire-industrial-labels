Kiss-Cut or Through-Cut Industrial Labels: Specify the Backing Too

Published November 11, 2026

An equipment builder wants one label in each installation kit. The quotation says die-cut, and the proof shows the correct outline. The shipment could still arrive as labels on a shared sheet rather than the separate backed pieces the kit packer expected. Specify the layers being cut and the unit being delivered.

Use a section drawing instead of the name alone

Avery's explanation of kiss-cut stickers distinguishes cutting the sticker layer while leaving the backing intact from cutting through sticker and backing to make individual pieces. That is useful terminology, but a converter may use die-cut more broadly. An agreed drawing is a better purchase specification than an assumed vocabulary.

Identify face stock, adhesive and release liner. Mark the intended cut boundary on each layer. State whether the surrounding face waste is removed and whether several labels remain on one carrier. A finished face outline does not define the size or shape of the backing supplied to the operator.

Required handoffWhat to put in the brief
Printer or applicator webContinuous carrier, finished face outline and equipment-approved web construction.
Manual sheetLabels per sheet, backing size, peeling access and sheet identification.
Individual kit piecesOne backed piece per handoff, its overall dimensions and packing count.
Tear-off sectionsSeparating feature and affected layers, reviewed under a separate perforation specification.

Count what the packer actually receives

For an illustrative kit needing three different labels, one sheet containing all three and three separate backed pieces can both represent three labels. They create different packing actions. Give the supplier the required count by design, pieces per kit and permitted grouping. If the carrier itself contains kit identification, include it in the approved drawing rather than treating it as expendable decoration.

Review a sample through receiving, picking and the actual peel-and-apply action. For machine-fed stock, the equipment owner must approve the complete carrier construction before a production trial. An intentionally separated piece is not automatically suitable for equipment designed around a continuous web.

Release the format after the sample handoff

Hold the order when the face dimensions are clear but the backing treatment or delivered count is unresolved. The converter should confirm feasibility and the buyer should approve the packing unit. This guide does not establish that every cut depth or format is available from CYJ.

If ordinary sheets already suit the kit process, special individual cutting may add no useful benefit. For custom industrial labels, send a layer drawing, face and backing dimensions, quantities by design and a photo of the kit handoff. Compare quotations only after they describe the same delivered construction. The shape guide covers the separate decision about the label's outside contour.
