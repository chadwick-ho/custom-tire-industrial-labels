Replacing a Tire Pallet Carrier Label: Preserve the SSCC for the Same Unit

Published November 8, 2026

A dispatch team has already identified a wrapped tire pallet and linked it to an electronic shipment record. The carrier changes before collection. The transport segment needs replacement, and the team must decide whether to preserve the pallet's existing identity or refer the change to the owner.

The GS1 Logistic Label Guideline, section 6.4, describes preserving the original SSCC when the carrier segment is replaced during the logistic unit's journey. The guidance allows the SSCC-bearing segment to stay in place or the original SSCC to be reproduced. First establish whether the identified physical unit has stayed the same.

Identify what changed at the loading bay

If the unit is intact, confirm with dispatch which carrier information changed and which identifier remains tied to the unit. If the pallet was split, rebuilt or combined with another, ask the logistics data owner to determine the identity and electronic records for the changed physical units. A decision about a changed unit involves more than printing another carrier segment.

Dispatch observationReview before reprinting
Same intact unit, carrier changedKeep the assigned SSCC and replace the approved carrier information.
Old segment obscures the valid IDAgree placement and how the legitimate SSCC remains readable.
Contents or unit grouping changedAsk the logistics owner to resolve physical and electronic identities before release.
Existing ID is uncertainHold the job while dispatch checks the assigned source and physical pallet.

Check the receiving record as well as the print

Keep one reprint record showing the pallet reference, original SSCC, changed carrier segment, approved source and disposition of superseded routing information. Verify the readable and encoded SSCC against the approved dispatch record. Then have the receiving or logistics owner confirm the current carrier data are reflected in the documents or electronic messages they use. Ensure obsolete routing information cannot direct the pallet to the wrong shipment.

The wrapped pallet guide covers placement and the initial decision about which unit to identify. If a workflow uses an internal pallet identifier, follow the owner's approved change rules and trading-partner instructions for that scheme.

For custom barcode label artwork, send the approved segment layout, assigned data format, dimensions, wrap application and print quantities. Confirm who owns the carrier change and the shipping record before a replacement is printed.
