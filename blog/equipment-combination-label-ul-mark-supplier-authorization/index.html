Equipment Combination Labels: Check UL Mark Supplier Authorization Before Ordering

Published November 24, 2026

An equipment buyer asks one printer to combine the product ratings and a UL Certification Mark on a single adhesive label. A quotation mentioning recognized label material leaves the supplier-authorization question open. Resolve that question before circulating a production-ready mark file.

Define the combined label before selecting its supply route

Have the end-product owner identify the exact approved marking requirement and who controls its use. Distinguish a label containing ordinary identification text from one carrying a Certification Mark. A blank space on a drawing does not authorize a printer to fill it with a copied logo.

For a proposed combined label, request the legal name and relevant production location of the actual mark supplier, its authorization reference and the route for confirming current coverage. A trading company's name on a quotation may not identify the party producing the mark. Ask who will perform each part of the job and who releases the finished artwork.

Review the label construction on a separate track. Authorization to supply marks does not establish that an arbitrary film, ink, adhesive and finish meet the end-product marking requirement. Equally, evidence supporting a marking system is not permission for any printer to produce the Certification Mark.

Keep the product owner's approved mark artwork and marking instructions under its control. If purchasing proposes separate labels or another production route, return that proposal to the owner for approval; splitting the drawing is not a workaround for authorization. Do not issue a usable certification-mark proof while the route remains unresolved.

UL Solutions’ marking and labeling FAQ distinguishes its marking-systems program from its Authorized Label Supplier program and addresses labels combining a Certification Mark with required safety information. Use its current program instructions and the end-product owner’s requirements for the actual order.

What to include in the approval record

Review pointEvidence to keep
Content scopeOwner identifies the combined label and approved marking requirement.
Supplier routeActual producing entity, location, current authorization evidence and confirmation route.
Construction routeEvidence for the proposed finished marking system and intended conditions.
ReleaseOwner-approved artwork reference, responsible approver and unresolved restrictions.

Before approving the order

Hold this purchase route when the supplier identity or authorization cannot be confirmed. A general quality certificate and a familiar logo in an email do not resolve the missing evidence. Ask the product owner and relevant program contact to settle the route before committing to production or a delivery date.

This guide does not claim that CYJ is an Authorized Label Supplier or offer authorization to reproduce a Certification Mark. For an identification label without such a mark, specify the approved ordinary content and relevant material requirements through the normal review.
