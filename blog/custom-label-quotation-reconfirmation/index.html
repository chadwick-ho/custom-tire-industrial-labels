Using an Earlier Label Quote? Reconfirm the Revision and Assumptions

Published November 4, 2026

Purchasing has a useful label quotation from an earlier enquiry. The artwork has since changed, the SKU mix is smaller and the requested destination is different. Copying the old unit price into a new order may leave the supplier and buyer referring to different jobs.

Before using an earlier quotation, identify the offer reference and ask the supplier to reconfirm it against the current specification and requested schedule. Record any stated validity conditions and changed assumptions. This guide offers a comparison record; it does not decide the legal effect of a quotation or set a CYJ price-validity period.

Name the offer and the job it described

A saved amount is less useful than a retrievable quotation with its date, revision, currency, quantities and specification references. Find the source offer rather than relying on a copied spreadsheet cell or a screenshot without context.

Then identify the current tire or industrial label requirement. Keep the finished size, shape, material direction, adhesive requirement, finish, approved artwork, data workflow and roll or sheet format together. If a field in the old quote was provisional, leave that history visible.

Avery's custom-label quote guidance illustrates how dimensions, material, quantities by design and other requirements enter its quotation process. It also distinguishes a general custom-shape estimate from pricing based on the final requirements. That is an example from its service, not a CYJ pricing rule.

Send a change list the supplier can answer

Use three columns: quoted basis, current request and supplier confirmation. Avoid replacing the earlier document without explaining what changed.

  • SKU quantities: a smaller total, more versions or a different quantity distribution can represent a different production request.
  • Construction and finish: identify any proposed change instead of calling both versions the same sticker.
  • Artwork and data: state the current revision, barcode or serial-data requirements and approval status.
  • Finished format: compare shape, core, unwind, labels per roll and any changed equipment requirement.
  • Delivery and timing: confirm the destination, scope and required receiving event, including assumptions still awaiting review.

Where nothing has changed, say that explicitly and still ask whether the supplier confirms the identified offer for the current request. Where a difference exists, ask which lines need revision rather than assuming the whole quotation must be discarded.

Keep price, timing and supply availability distinct

A response confirming a price does not necessarily answer whether a particular construction is available for the required schedule. Likewise, an available production slot does not identify the final quoted amount. Request the separate confirmations the order actually needs.

If the earlier offer states a validity date or other conditions, retain them in the review. If their meaning is unclear or the time has passed, request a current written response from the supplier. Do not invent a universal number of days for which all custom-label prices remain valid.

The custom-order policy notes that changes after approval can affect cost, timing or feasibility. For an urgent request, use the delivery-planning guide to confirm milestones alongside the quote review.

Resolve alternatives before the order instruction

A supplier may offer an alternative material, roll format or quantity basis. Keep it on a separate option line with the differences visible. A technically unapproved alternative should not replace the original specification just because its price fits an old purchasing target.

Record which confirmed offer and specification the buyer intends to use. If a revised quotation replaces an earlier one, make the active reference easy for the person issuing the order to find. Preserve the earlier document as history rather than treating two different revisions as interchangeable approvals.

Prepare a quotation reconfirmation brief with the earlier offer reference, current label specifications, quantities per SKU, changed fields and required destination and timing. Send the supporting offer privately through the site's existing contact route.
