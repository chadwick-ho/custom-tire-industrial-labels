Tire Pallet Labels: Distinguish the SSCC From Shipment and Consignment References

Published November 16, 2026

A tire distributor receives a pallet label template with spaces for a unit ID, a shipment reference and a carrier reference. The test team reads the carrier reference when it intends to receive one physical pallet. Agree the role of each field before ordering the released print layout.

Map each reference to the object or grouping it identifies

Have the dispatch and carrier teams identify the reference used for the physical logistic unit and those used for the shipment or consignment. Where the parties use GS1, the SSCC identifies the logistic unit. A Global Shipment Identification Number describes a shipment, and a Global Identification Number for Consignment describes a grouping from the logistics provider’s perspective. Confirm current field and carrier requirements with the receiving owner.

Use a witness with at least two separately identified pallets under one shipment reference, if that is representative of the operation. Check which scan receives an individual pallet and which record only provides context. Preserve the matching electronic dispatch record and test the agreed handling when a carrier groups the units differently. A routine buyer order number can remain a separate administrative field.

GS1’s explanation of GSIN and GINC distinguishes the seller-to-buyer shipment from the way logistics providers group units for transport. Use the receiver’s agreed specification for every field.

Review pointEvidence to keep
UnitOne named physical pallet and its assigned unit identifier.
GroupingShipment and consignment references and their owners.
ArtworkClear field roles, approved data and intended scan positions.
WitnessTwo actual recipient views that show unit and higher-level grouping.

Before approving the order

Hold if reading the shipment or carrier field is treated as confirmation of an individual pallet. If the parties only use a private delivery reference, document its role rather than assigning it a GS1 meaning by appearance.

Related reading includes the changed-carrier label guide, the physical group identification guide, the same-pallet two-face guide.

For tire barcode labels, send the unit, shipment and consignment field map, approved sample values and the receiver’s expected scan action.
