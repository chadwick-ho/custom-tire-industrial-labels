Temporary Equipment Labels: When to Specify an Adhesive-Free Removal Tab

Published November 11, 2026

A temporary inspection label must stay on an equipment housing through handling, then come off at a defined station. The approved sample lifts acceptably once an edge is raised, but operators cannot easily start the peel without touching the finish. A grip area may solve access while leaving the adhesive performance question separate.

Define the feature on the applied label

Mark a proposed unbonded tab and the area intended to hold the label in place. An aid that helps release a sticker from its liner does not necessarily remain available after the label is pressed onto the equipment. State that this tab is intended for the later removal step.

HERMA's banderole example describes adhesive-free grip tabs for opening that specific product. It demonstrates a possible converting feature. It does not establish availability for this equipment label, suitability for a painted surface or an accepted removal result.

Sample checkQuestion to answer
Before useIs the tab where the approved drawing puts it after liner removal and application?
During handlingDoes the label stay attached and readable without the tab snagging?
At removalCan the intended worker reach the grip area using the authorized process?
After removalDoes the actual surface meet the owner's agreed residue and finish criteria?

Do not solve a chemistry issue with a geometry change

An accessible tab may help start a peel. It does not establish acceptable peel force, residue or surface appearance. Review those outcomes on the representative finish after the real dwell and exposure. If the ordinary removable construction already offers an accessible edge, a special tab may add no benefit.

If the tab can snag or sits against a rib, discuss a different location or a different label format. Preserve the intended label information and bonded area in the review. If the label is permanent or carries a required warning or rating, do not apply this temporary-removal concept without the responsible owner's separate decision.

Confirm the conversion and removal together

Hold the order when grip access or the post-removal finish remains unresolved. Have the converter confirm feasibility and the equipment owner approve the actual handling and removal. Use the adhesive lifetime guide to define why and when the label should come off.

For temporary equipment identification, send the surface photo, marked bonded and unbonded areas, intended dwell, removal conditions, label dimensions and quantities.
