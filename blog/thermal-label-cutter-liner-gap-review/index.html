Thermal Label Cutters: Put the Cut Line in the Approved Liner Gap

Published November 11, 2026

A maintenance-label job needs separate pieces at the printer outlet. The media fits the printer and the text prints correctly, so the buyer assumes its cutter can cut anywhere on the web. The cutter's permitted materials are another acceptance condition.

Identify the installed cutter option and the layers present at its intended cut line. A standard liner cutter and a purpose-designed linerless cutter are different configurations. Do not infer adhesive-cutting capability from a product photograph or from the word cutter on an order.

Read the instructions for the installed option

The Zebra ZD620/ZD420 user guide distinguishes its standard media cutter from its linerless option. For the standard cutter it describes cutting label liner and light tag media and excludes cutting labels, adhesive or embedded circuitry. Its limits apply to the described configurations; they are not universal specifications for other equipment.

Have the equipment owner identify the applicable option, manual and permitted media. If that evidence is missing, retain the cutter question as unresolved. A supply that has run on another printer does not establish compatibility here.

ItemRelease question
Installed hardwareWhich cutter part or option and manual govern this machine?
Cut positionWhich layers cross the proposed line after the normal feed operation?
Delivered mediaAre gap, width, thickness and cut interval within the applicable limits?
Normal jobDoes the released template and equipment setup put each cut in the approved location?

Show the gap and cut line on the same drawing

Mark the label edges, feed direction, exposed-liner region and intended separation line. Include any printed marks, adhesive extensions or special inserts affecting that region. Ask the converter to confirm the supplied geometry and the equipment owner to assess positioning through the actual job.

A wide-looking blank area on the face artwork is not necessarily adhesive-free. The construction drawing must show what is physically present. Likewise, a screen preview does not establish where the installed machine will cut after feeding or backfeeding.

Choose a supported handoff

Keep the supply on hold if the intended line crosses material the installed cutter does not permit. Discuss a supported media construction, an approved different output arrangement or suitable separate preparation. Changing hardware or operation belongs to the equipment owner. This article supplies no blade adjustment or maintenance procedure.

A representative approved trial should retain the media identity, job reference, cut position and separated piece. Readable data and clean separation are distinct observations. The perforated-liner guide covers a different route in which the operator tears the carrier.

For industrial labels printed locally, send the printer and cutter details, applicable media limits, marked cut-line drawing, finished label size and quantity. Approve the cutter/media combination before relying on individually separated output.
