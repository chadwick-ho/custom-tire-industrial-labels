Equipment Cut-Letter Markings: Specify What the Installer Removes

Published November 13, 2026

A machine panel design needs individual letters with no visible background film. The drawing shows the final spacing, but the installation team needs the letters to arrive as one aligned set. Ask the converter what remains on the panel after installation and what the operator removes.

Review the supplied layers and the final panel together

Identify the cut marking, rear release liner and any temporary front application carrier. Give the supplier the approved letter positions and the panel’s installation reference. Compare a witness installed on the actual finish with the approved drawing. Inspect alignment after carrier removal and check that small parts remain attached.

Consider whether a single printed label would meet the same appearance and legibility requirement. It may be simpler when a continuous face is acceptable or the lettering is small. If separated elements are essential, the converter should confirm feasible details and the proposed process before the buyer commits to a quantity.

ORAFOL’s MT80P application tape product page identifies transfer of cut lettering and symbols as an application for that specific film. Ask the converter to confirm the carrier for the chosen marking and the actual panel finish.

Review pointEvidence to keep
DrawingExact spacing, finished shapes and an installation datum on the equipment panel.
Supplied setWhich liner or carrier is supplied, approved construction and handling reference.
Installed sampleAlignment, retained pieces, appearance after carrier removal and acceptance owner.

Before approving the order

Hold when the final spacing is undefined or the sample lifts with the temporary carrier. Have the converter and equipment owner approve the actual finished panel. A continuous printed label can remain the preferred format when the visual requirement allows it.

For equipment machinery labels, send the cut artwork, panel finish and datum, smallest features, installation conditions and quantities.
