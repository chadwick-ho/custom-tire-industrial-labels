Shorter EAN/UPC Tire Labels Need a Retail-Only Scope Review

Published November 21, 2026

A tire retail label has limited vertical space, and the designer has heard that EAN/UPC bars may now be shorter. Before using that allowance, trace the label's actual scan path. A symbol needed at both checkout and a distribution station cannot be assessed as though its only task is retail point of sale.

Confirm the rule adopted by the receiving program

Record the exact symbology, required scanning environments, applicable specification and the party approving it. Keep a full-height layout as the starting comparison and document why the available area cannot accommodate it. Look first at whether nonessential copy, placement or the label boundary can change without compromising the required information.

GS1 change notification 26-144 addresses EAN/UPC height reduction for general retail POS without general distribution. GS1's change directory lists it for incorporation into version 27.0. GS1 New Zealand separately reports adoption from 16 October 2026 and describes reduction by up to 60% of the relevant minimum height in its stated circumstances. That is a conditional limit, not an instruction to remove 60% from every tire barcode.

Have the receiving owner confirm its applicable adoption and customer requirements before releasing the geometry. Do not carry this EAN/UPC provision to GS1-128, ITF-14 or another carrier. Keep the X-dimension, clear margins, readable data and other applicable requirements under their own review. If a reduced height is approved, retain the greatest practical height under that decision and qualify the finished printed label at the actual checkout presentation. Obtain any required verification separately.

GS1’s EAN/UPC truncation change notification provides the detailed change. Compare it with GS1 New Zealand’s adoption and scope notice. Neither source approves an individual tire layout or establishes universal partner adoption.

What to include in the approval record

Review pointEvidence to keep
ScopeActual carrier and every required scan environment.
AuthorityApplicable specification, adoption and receiving-owner decision.
GeometryFull-height alternative, space constraint and approved physical dimensions.
WitnessFinished print, required verification and actual checkout reading task.

Before approving the order

Hold the shortened version if any required scan environment or applicable rule remains unresolved. A working full-height label does not need redesign merely because an allowance exists. A warehouse-only Code 128 job follows a different specification. Do not crop an existing barcode image as a substitute for controlled artwork generation.

Related reading includes the overall tire-label size brief, the separate UPC-E eligibility guide, the verification and operational scan guide.

For tire barcode labels, send the current full-height artwork, space constraint, approved GTIN and carrier, complete scan path and receiving specification.
