A PVN Request on Tire Labels Needs an AI 22 Mapping Before Print

Published November 21, 2026

A tire buyer asks for a PVN field in a new data handoff, while the supplier's schema still calls AI 22 CPV. Do not assign a fresh code or silently copy the artwork revision into that column. First agree the field's meaning, the approved variation value and the implementation the receiver will use.

Release one field contract across the two names

Have the brand or product-data owner classify the variation and provide the approved GTIN and associated value. Ask the receiver which Application Identifier and data schema its request names. A drawing revision, internal SKU suffix, batch and unit serial are not interchangeable just because each can be placed in a text column.

GS1 change notification 25-223 renames AI 22 from consumer product variant to product variation number and extends its described use beyond consumer products. The notification identifies a September 2026 change, while GS1's directory lists incorporation in version 27.0. Identify the applicable adopted specification and partner implementation rather than assuming that a changed heading proves all systems have migrated.

Document the source-column mapping, exact approved values, GTIN relationship and expected parsed receiving fields. Use harmless witness records for the relevant variations. Compare the printed readable information, actual encoded data and receiving interpretation. If a legacy column and a new column name refer to the same approved attribute, preserve that mapping explicitly instead of creating two competing data sources.

The variation attribute does not authorize keeping a GTIN when the actual product change requires another GTIN. Keep that allocation decision with its owner. The GS1 terminology update also does not by itself determine a tire's legal digital-product-passport obligations or a compliance deadline; a particular regulatory project needs its own qualified scope review.

GS1’s product-variation change notification defines the AI 22 naming and scope change. Its status appears in the GS1 change directory. Confirm the applicable implementation with the data and receiving owners.

What to include in the approval record

Review pointEvidence to keep
MeaningRequired AI, approved variation meaning and its GTIN relationship.
VersionAdopted specification, schema names and receiving implementation.
WitnessExact source values, encoded fields and actual parsed result.
AuthorityNamed owner of the variation, GTIN and artwork release.

Before approving the order

Hold if PVN is merely an unexplained new column or a printer is asked to invent its value. A plain internal artwork-revision label can retain its own approved field where AI 22 is not required. Keep any DPP or market-compliance approval separate from reproduction of an agreed barcode field.

For tire barcode labels, send the receiver's field dictionary, adopted GS1 reference, approved GTIN/variation examples, artwork and quantities by version.
