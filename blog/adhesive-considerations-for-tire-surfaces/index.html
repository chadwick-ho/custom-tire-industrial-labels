Adhesive Considerations for Tire Surfaces

Tire surfaces can be challenging for labels. Adhesive options should be discussed according to surface texture, application conditions and the intended labeling process.

Quote Checklist

  • Label type and application
  • Surface and environment
  • Size, shape and quantity
  • Material and adhesive preference
  • Barcode, QR code or variable data
  • Artwork or existing label reference
Send Requirements

No Universal Tire Adhesive

Do not assume one adhesive is suitable for every tire application. Tire type, surface texture, contamination, storage and handling conditions can affect selection.

What to Tell Your Supplier

Send tire surface details, label size, application method, indoor or outdoor use, transport requirements and any sample evaluation needs.

When Samples Help

For applications with specific surface or environmental requirements, sample evaluation may be recommended before bulk production.
