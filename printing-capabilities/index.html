Printing Options

Projects may include full color printing, spot color printing, black and white printing, custom text, custom graphics, product codes and identification information. Available printing methods and specifications should be confirmed according to each project.

Barcode and QR Code Printing

Barcode and QR code labels can be discussed for tire inventory, equipment identification, asset tracking, warehouse labels and product identification. Please send the code type, data source, label size and scanner or workflow requirements if applicable.

Sequential Numbering and Serial Numbers

Sequential numbering and serial number labels can be discussed for asset tracking, product identification, equipment ID and batch control. Please provide the numbering rule or data file.

Finishing and Converting

Available finishing and converting options can be confirmed according to your label specification. Options may include matte finish, gloss finish, lamination, protective lamination, die cut, custom shape, roll labels and sheet labels.

Proof Before Production

A digital proof helps confirm artwork layout, size, text, barcode placement and visual details before production. It does not verify adhesion, durability, chemical resistance, temperature performance or regulatory compliance.
