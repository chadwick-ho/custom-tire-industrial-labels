What this website helps buyers specify

CYJ Labels helps business buyers define custom tire and industrial label projects. Use this site to compare applications, prepare a specification and discuss your requirements through our published contact routes.

Project scope

Tire labels cover visible tire information and defined data workflows. Industrial labels cover equipment, container, warning and tracking applications. Exact construction and supply terms require project review.

The custom label process connects requirements, artwork proof and production discussion.

Approval responsibilities

The buyer supplies approved product information, artwork and identifier data. Where technical, hazard or certification content is involved, its responsible owner confirms the content and authorization. A print proof checks the supplied layout; it does not certify a product or establish all material performance.

Buyer guides explain decisions and link to original technical sources where relevant. Illustrative examples and AI-generated applications are identified as examples rather than customer orders or test results.
