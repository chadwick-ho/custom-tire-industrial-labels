What Samples Can Help Review

Samples can help review label size and shape, artwork layout, general material appearance, printing information, roll or sheet format and application discussion.

What Samples Do Not Automatically Prove

A sample does not automatically confirm long-term adhesion, chemical resistance, outdoor durability, temperature performance or regulatory compliance unless a specific test and specification are agreed in advance.

Information Needed for Sample Discussion

Please send label type, size, quantity, material preference, adhesive requirement, surface, environment, artwork and any special testing requirement.
