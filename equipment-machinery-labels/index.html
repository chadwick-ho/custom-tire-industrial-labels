CYJ Labels · Buyer decision guide

Define the equipment label’s installed job

Specify whether the label identifies the machine, gives operating information or carries approved warnings. Mark the final surface and viewing position before selecting construction. A removable guard, a temporary protective film and the permanent machine body are different attachment points.

Decisions to settle before requesting production
DecisionWhat to defineInput or evidence
Stable identificationWhich physical unit the ID followsApproved equipment record and intended attachment point
Viewing and accessWhere staff read or scan the installed informationPanel photo, direction and normal use position
Technical contentOwner-approved wording, symbols and valuesEngineering or safety approval reference as applicable

Checks to keep separate

Printing a machine label does not approve an equipment rating or safety instruction. Use content released by the responsible product owner.

Prepare the buying brief

Prepare panel photos and marked position, surface finish, dimensions, approved content and data, exposure, installation stage and quantity.

Prepare My Requirements; then share supporting files through the existing email or WhatsApp route.

Resolve a specific buying question

  • Identify the final surface before application
  • Keep equipment identity separate from removable parts
  • Approve the viewing and adhesive faces of window labels

Separate a machine label set from an asset number run

A machine label set may contain several different messages for one equipment model. An asset-label run may use one layout with a different identifier for every unit. Tell the buyer and artwork approver which pattern applies so the quote separates design count from unique-data count.

For a multi-label set, mark each position on an equipment photo and give every design a reference. Have the responsible owner confirm the current wording and model version. A set can be complete on paper and still be difficult to place correctly without that position map.
