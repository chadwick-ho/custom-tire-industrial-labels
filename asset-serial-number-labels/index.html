For Asset Tracking and Identification

Asset labels help identify and track equipment, tools, inventory and fixed assets. Serial number labels support product identification, batch control and internal management workflows.

Variable Information Options

Asset and serial number labels can use fixed data or variable data supplied by the customer.

  • Sequential numbers
  • Unique serial numbers
  • Asset ID codes
  • Product codes
  • Model numbers
  • Batch numbers
  • Barcodes
  • QR codes
  • Different text per label

Data File Requirements

For variable data projects, please send your numbering rule or upload an Excel / CSV file. Include required prefixes, suffixes, barcode content and human-readable text.
