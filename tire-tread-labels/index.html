Tire Tread Labels

Tire tread labels are used to display tire information, specifications, barcodes, QR codes, branding and product identification on the tread area. Because tire tread labeling involves curved and potentially challenging surfaces, material and adhesive options should be selected according to the tire surface and application process.

Information Commonly Printed

Tire tread labels can carry product names, tire sizes, specification details, product codes, barcodes, QR codes, batch information and product information.

  • Brand or product name
  • Tire size and specification
  • Product code
  • Barcode or QR code
  • Batch or inventory information

Material and Adhesive Note

Tire tread applications should be reviewed project by project. We do not assume one adhesive is suitable for every tire surface. Tell us your tire surface and labeling conditions so suitable options can be discussed.

FAQ

Questions Buyers Ask

Can you make tire tread labels with barcodes?

Yes. Barcode and QR code printing can be discussed for tire tread label projects.

Can you guarantee adhesion on every tire?

No. Tire surfaces and application conditions vary. Adhesive options should be evaluated according to the actual surface and labeling process.
