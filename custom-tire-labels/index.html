What to Confirm Before Quotation

Please send label size, shape, quantity, tire surface, application method, required material, adhesive preference, barcode or QR code requirement, variable data requirement, roll or sheet format and artwork if available.

Material and Adhesive Discussion

Material and adhesive options can be selected according to the tire surface and application requirements. No single construction should be assumed to work on every tire surface.

Tell us your tire surface, label size, application method and production requirements so available options can be discussed.

Printing and Variable Data

Tire labels may include full color graphics, product text, barcode printing, QR code printing, sequential numbering, batch information, product codes and other variable data. Please send your data file, numbering rule or sample label for review.

Roll and Sheet Formats

Tire labels are commonly discussed in roll format for production and application workflows. Sheet format may be discussed where applicable. Core size, roll size and winding direction should be confirmed according to your labeling process.
